YOUR STEP-BY-STEP GUIDE

How to Use WIN Business Suite

Follow these simple steps to set up your business and manage your daily activities.

01

Set Up Your Business

Start by entering your business information, including its name and other important details.

Example: Enter your shop name, business type and contact details.

Set up your business →
02

Add Your Products to Inventory

Open Inventory and add the products your business sells. Enter each product's name and the required product details.

Example: Add sugar, cooking oil, rice or any product your shop sells.

Open Inventory →
03

Set Buying and Selling Prices

Enter how much you pay to obtain each product and how much you charge your customers.

Example: If you buy a product for KSh 80 and sell it for KSh 100, enter KSh 80 as the buying price and KSh 100 as the selling price.

Set product prices →
04

Enter Product Quantities

Record how many units of each product are currently available. Set a minimum stock level to help identify products that need attention.

Example: Enter 50 units as the available quantity and 5 units as the minimum stock level.

Manage quantities →
05

Record Your Sales

Use Sales & POS whenever a customer buys something. Select the product, enter the quantity and record the payment.

Example: A customer buys 2 products at KSh 100 each. Record the sale and the amount received.

Open Sales & POS →
06

Track Your Business Expenses

Record money spent on running your business so that you can understand your costs.

Example: Record rent, electricity, transport or other business expenses.

07

Understand Your Business Overview

Use the figures on your Dashboard to follow your sales, profit, expenses and inventory value.

Tip: Check your figures regularly to understand how your business is performing.

08

Use WIN AI

WIN AI is intended to help you understand your business information and make smarter business decisions.

Example: Ask for help understanding business performance or identifying areas that need attention.
